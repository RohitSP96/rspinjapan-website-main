Our partner institution 提携校

Yokohama Design College / Yokohama language school

We send our N3-and-above students to Yokohama language school because of its strong track record with international students, its structured route through N2 and N1, and its location — close enough to Tokyo to open doors, grounded enough to actually study.

It's also a campus we know first-hand. Rohit stays in touch with faculty and alumni there, which means our students arrive already understood, not as strangers starting from zero.

Admission & visa process 入学・ビザ手続き

Eight steps, one destination.

1

Counselling

We map your current level, timeline, and goals, and set a realistic study plan toward Yokohama.

2

Language training

Structured classes from your starting level up through N3, with admission requirements built into the syllabus.

3

Document preparation

Academic records, financial documents, and application forms — checked line by line before submission.

4

Application

Your application goes to Yokohama language school with our guidance on timing and intake windows.

5

Certificate of Eligibility (COE)

We track the COE process with the school and help you respond quickly to any requests.

6

Visa

Visa application support and interview preparation, so you walk in ready.

7

Departure

Travel planning, packing guidance, and a final check-in before you fly.

8

Arrival support

Help settling in — from your first days in Yokohama to finding your footing as a student in Japan.

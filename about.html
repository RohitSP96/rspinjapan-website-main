Founder 代表

Rohit Shiv Prasad

Founder & Head Instructor, based in Kyoto

Rohit moved from India to Kyoto to live and work in Japan — and quickly realised how much the journey from a Meerut classroom to a Japanese workplace was missing a guide. RSP in Japan started as a promise to give students exactly what he wished he'd had: an instructor who has actually lived every stage of the path.

His teaching philosophy treats Japanese as more than vocabulary and grammar drills. Keigo, workplace etiquette, and the quiet discipline of daily practice are taught alongside the JLPT syllabus, because that's what a real classroom in Japan — and a real Japanese employer — expects.

About the company 会社概要

Five years, one direction: India to Japan.

RSP in Japan was founded in 2020. In that time we've sent more than 50 students to study at Yokohama language school, taken them from N3 through to N1, and watched several of them secure job placements in Japan.

2020

RSP in Japan founded

Rohit starts teaching Japanese from Kyoto, with the first classes running for students back in Meerut.

2021

First student batch

The first cohort completes N5–N4, and the pathway to Yokohama language school takes real shape.

2022–23

First N1 achievements

Early students reach N1, proving the full N5-to-N1 pathway works outside Japan, not just inside it.

2024–25

50+ students milestone

More than fifty students have now studied with RSP and moved on to Yokohama language school.

Today

A growing bridge between Meerut and Japan

New batches start regularly, with graduates now working in Japan and mentoring the students behind them.
